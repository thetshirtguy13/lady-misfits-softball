Fastpitch words used in the lessons
Look-back rule
A runner must promptly advance or return when the pitcher controls the ball in the circle and play settles.
Slap
A moving left-handed hitting technique used to place or drive the ball.
DP/FLEX
Linked lineup roles with specific offensive, defensive, substitution, and re-entry rules.
Wheel
A bunt defense where the corners charge and the middle infield rotates.
